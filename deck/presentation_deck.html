Finding 1: Onboarding Funnel Bleed

Overall onboarding conversion is only 16.82% (4,206 approved / 25,000 signups). The single largest volume loss is Registration Certificate (RC) Stage 3, where 6,102 captains (29.35% of all losses) drop out—primarily due to mechanical photo blur and OCR errors on low-tier smartphones.

Finding 2: Campaign WhatsApp Reality

Campaign CAMP_WA_002's claimed +17.90% lift is confounded by immortal-time bias (100% of recipients had already survived DL+RC). The true stage-matched observational lift is +4.48% pts (95% CI: +3.12% to +5.84% pts), with 0% clicker advantage. Do NOT scale 5x without an RCT.

Finding 3: Airport Nocturnal Deficit

Airport shortage is 77.51% nocturnal (21:00–03:00) with 42,673 lost trips/mo. Daytime is 96.90% fulfilled. 41.07% of trips drop in suburbs where return fares collapse to 11.06% (88.94% stranded rate), driving a 24.50% cancellation rate.

The Big Business Decision

Do NOT fund broad airport captain acquisition. The airport is an open leaky bucket exporting drivers to suburbs. Instead, deploy Direct VAHAN API Auto-Fetch, Controlled Campaign RCT, and Forward-Dispatch & Suburban Return Subsidies.

Slide 4 / 6

The Fatal Immortal-Time Bias

The naive claim (34.72% vs 16.82% → +17.90% pts) compares drivers who already survived DL and RC hurdles (100% of treated) against raw signups who dropped out at Step 1. This is fundamentally comparing apples to oranges.

Stage-Matched Observational Evidence

Comparing treated drivers against controls who also cleared DL+RC yields 28.51% vs 24.14% (+4.37% pts). Logistic regression adjusting for city, vehicle, channel, device tier, age, and month yields +4.48% pts (95% CI: +3.12% to +5.84% pts).

The Complier Test & Business Decision

  • Zero Clicker Advantage: Drivers who clicked the WhatsApp link converted at 28.24% vs 28.70% for non-clickers. Nudge acted as passive awareness.
  • Decision: REJECT 5x budget scale. Launch a 50/50 RCT (N=3,305/arm) testing an automated 2-way failure-resolution bot before committing capital.

Airport Supply & Marketplace Economics
77.51% of Deficit is Nocturnal: Suburban Deadhead Risk Drives Supply Avoidance
Slide 5 / 6

Temporal Shortage (21:00–03:00)

  • 42,673 Lost Trips / Month (77.51% of all unmet airport demand).
  • Fulfillment crashes to 47.20% (26.24% at 23:00).
  • Surge averages 2.17x and ETAs blow out to 10.0 minutes.
  • Daytime (04:00–20:00) is 96.90% fulfilled (Acquisition is redundant).

The Suburban Deadhead Trap

  • 41.07% of Airport Trips Drop in Suburbs (SUB-07, SUB-11).
  • 88.94% Night Stranded Rate: Only 11.06% get a return fare in 20 min.
  • Drivers face 30 km of unpaid return driving, driving night cancellations to 24.50% (vs 11.20% for CBD drops).

Why Localized Airport Acquisition Fails

Acquiring drivers living near the airport treats the terminal as a silo. Within 48 hours, acquired drivers take suburban trips, get stranded, and leave the catchment empty. The airport is a leaky bucket.
